PSOLA notes
- Pitch-Synchronous Overlap-Add
- Change the fundamental frequency of a speaker's voice without changing other qualities (only changing pitch, not the more personal parts of a person singing like timbre)
- PSOLA is also able to do speech synthesis (TTS) and voice conversion
- Uses STFT, which is DTFT/FFT over small chunks of the signal at a time (window), because normal DTFT
	- For STFT, multiply a window function in while taking the DFT of a signal

How PSOLA works
1. Find Pitch periods or Pitch Epochs
	- This can be done by looking at peaks in the speech waveform
	- Or you can use FFT to find fundamental freq. then infer epochs
	- These mark the start and stop of one pitch period
2. Once we find the pitch period, we can stretch/squeeze it by a factor x to make longer/shorter periods which result in lower/higher pitch
3. Once we have these pitch periods, we can create a "new" audio file, but we must window them so they blend smoothly
